About Me

I have always been interested in technology and computers from a young age. However, due to different circumstances, I did not have the opportunity to pursue a technology-related path earlier.

I continued my studies in other fields and later completed my Master's degree in Economics in Germany. As technology has become an increasingly important part of almost every field, I started thinking about exploring my long-standing interest in technology more seriously.

This motivated me to make a career change and begin studying software development. I am now at the beginning of this new journey, learning new skills and developing my knowledge step by step.

My Skills

HTML

Proficiency: Beginner

Before starting this course, I only knew that HTML was used to create websites. Apart from that, I did not really understand how HTML worked, how a webpage was structured, or how different HTML elements were used. I started learning HTML as a complete beginner.

Through this portfolio project, I am gradually learning how to use headings, paragraphs, sections, links, images, forms, and other HTML elements to build and organize a webpage.

CSS

Proficiency: Beginner

Before starting this course, I had no previous knowledge of CSS. It was completely new to me, and I started learning it from the very beginning.

While working on this portfolio, I have gradually started to understand how CSS is used to style a webpage, including changing colors, spacing, layout, and the appearance of different elements. I am still a beginner, but I am learning step by step through practice.

My Experience

Starting My Learning Journey

I started studying Software Engineering last month after previously studying in a completely different academic field. Because of this change in background, most of the concepts in software development are new to me, and I still find some parts challenging.

However, I am trying to learn step by step and gradually become more comfortable with the subject. My wife is also studying in the same field and started her studies last year. Seeing her journey and experience encouraged me and played an important role in inspiring me to choose this new path.

I hope that with time, practice, and continuous learning, I will be able to develop my skills and become more confident in software development.

Building My Portfolio

Building this portfolio website has been a completely new experience for me. When I started, I had very little knowledge about how to build a website, so I have been learning gradually throughout the process.

One of the most challenging parts for me has been understanding the code and learning which code is used for a particular purpose and how it affects the webpage. As a beginner, I still find coding challenging, but I am becoming more familiar with it through practice.

One part that I have found particularly interesting is CSS. I enjoy seeing how styling can change a simple webpage and make it more visually attractive and engaging for the person visiting the website.
